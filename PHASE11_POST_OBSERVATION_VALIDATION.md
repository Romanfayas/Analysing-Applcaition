# Phase 11: Post-Observation Validation

## Status: NOT YET OBSERVED

### Purpose
To validate the parity between expected signals and actual executed paper trades during live market observation.

### Validation Metrics
- **Signal Parity**: Do actual paper signals match expected Strategy V2 signals?
- **Entry Divergence**: Are trades entered at the expected bar?
- **Exit Divergence**: Are trades exited correctly based on the signal?
- **Execution Price Divergence**: How does the simulated paper price compare to the actual market tick?
- **Slippage**: Is the assumed 10 bps slippage model accurate?
- **Cost Divergence**: Are taxes and brokerages modelled accurately?
- **P&L Divergence**: Expected vs Realized P&L in the portfolio view.

*This report will be populated after the first actual market session observation.*
