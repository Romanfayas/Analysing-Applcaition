## 1. What is being tested
Whether, for Shariah-PASS Tier-1 candidates produced by the frozen Strategy V2 screener, the buy-stop trade defined in §3 earns a **positive mean net return in excess of a matched passive benchmark**. This is a single pre-specified hypothesis. No parameter, threshold, horizon, setup type, or filter is tuned or selected on outcomes.

## 2. Primary metric and benchmark
- **Primary metric:** mean over *independent* completed trades of `net_return_i − benchmark_return_i`.
- `net_return_i = net_pnl / (quantity × entry_fill)` from the itemized cost ledger (statutory + broker + modeled slippage) at a fixed paper notional of ₹1,00,000 per trade. Fixed charges (DP) are therefore *included* and matter at this notional.
- **Benchmark:** equal-weighted, cost-free buy-and-hold of the 47-symbol Tier-1 universe over the *same sessions as the trade* (close of the decision session → close of the exit session), computed from `ohlcv_daily`. Cost-free is deliberately unfavourable to the strategy. No index data source is currently stored, so an index benchmark is **not** used; this limitation is part of the spec.
- Secondary (descriptive only, no pass/fail): gross return, win rate with Wilson interval, payoff ratio, horizon marks at 1/3/5/10 sessions, distribution by setup type and regime.

## 3. Trade definition (deterministic)
- Decision is frozen at `candidate_timestamp` (timezone-aware). Only daily bars on sessions strictly after the decision session are consulted.
- **Entry:** buy-stop. Filled on the first of the next 10 expected sessions with `high ≥ entry_trigger`, at `max(open, entry_trigger)`. No fill in 10 sessions → `NOT_TRIGGERED` (no trade, no return, reported separately).
- **Exit:** stop `invalidation_price`, target `target_price`, or close of session 10 (`EXPIRED_TIME_STOP`). Opening gaps through a level fill at the open. If stop and target are both reachable on one session (including the entry session) the **stop** is taken. Daily bars cannot order intraday events; this rule is conservative and is not tuned.
- Horizon marks (1d, 3d, 5d, 10d = session index after decision) are recorded only while the position is open; otherwise the horizon status is `CLOSED_EARLY` / `NOT_ENTERED` / `PENDING`.

## 4. Observation statuses and inclusion rules
| status | meaning | in performance statistics? |
|---|---|---|
| `PENDING` | window not fully observed and no exit yet | no (counted as pending) |
| `COMPLETED` | exit reached, or window ended `NOT_TRIGGERED` | trades yes; `NOT_TRIGGERED` no return but reported with the entry-rate |
| `CENSORED` | terminal external event (delisting/suspension) before exit; reason recorded | no; counted and reported (sensitivity: bounding by a −100% exit is reported alongside) |
| `DATA_UNAVAILABLE` | a session that should have a bar has none/invalid bar | no; counted and reported |
| `INVALIDATED` | candidate not reproducible/valid (e.g. price inconsistent with stored market data) | no; counted and reported |

The counts of every status are published with every result so selective exclusion is visible. Corporate actions inside a window (split, bonus, demerger, rename) make the window `CENSORED` unless prices are verified adjusted; today `adjusted_close` is not stored, so such windows are censored, not silently included.

## 5. Independence, overlap and correlation
- **Candidate observations** (all rows) ≥ **completed trades** ≥ **independent observations**.
- Independent set: per symbol, greedy earliest-first, a trade is dropped if its decision session falls inside an already-kept trade's 10-session window.
- Cross-sectional correlation (many symbols breaking out on the same day in the same market) is **not** removed. To limit its influence, results are also reported with at most one trade per decision session (earliest by candidate timestamp, deterministic) as a sensitivity analysis. Both sets are shown; the primary uses the per-symbol independent set.

## 6. Sample-size gates (operational, not proof)
- At least **100 independent completed trades**, and at least **20 in each of ≥2 market regimes**, before a verdict other than `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` may be issued. Reaching the gates only *permits* a verdict; it does not imply significance.
- Sample sizes are reported separately for each horizon (1d/3d/5d/10d), each regime, each setup type, and for candidates / completed / independent.

## 7. Uncertainty
- 95% t-interval and a seeded percentile bootstrap (seed 20261014, 10,000 resamples) for the primary metric; Wilson 95% interval for win rates.
- With n < 2 no interval is reported (`null`). Any metric with n = 0 is `null`, never `0.00%`.

## 8. Drawdown
Computed on the chronological portfolio equity curve (initial capital ₹10,00,000; each trade ₹1,00,000; costs booked at exit; open positions marked at close from stored session closes), as peak-to-trough decline. Never from summed or averaged trade returns. The curve is reconciled to the sum of ledger net P&L after the last exit (tolerance ₹0.005).

## 9. Regimes and periods
Regime label is the one recorded at decision time (immutable). Results are reported per regime and per calendar quarter. No regime is dropped after the fact.

## 10. Verdicts (exhaustive)
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` — gates in §6 not met.
- `FAILED_VALIDATION` — gates met and the upper 95% bound (t **and** bootstrap) of the primary metric is < 0.
- `PASSED_PREREGISTERED_CRITERIA` — gates met and the lower 95% bound (t **and** bootstrap) is > 0. This is *not* a statement of profitability, robustness or live-trading readiness; those require separate review (liquidity/capacity, licensed real-time data, regime coverage, operational controls).
- `INCONCLUSIVE` — gates met, interval includes 0.
Live trading remains disabled under every verdict.

## 11. Anti-curve-fitting commitments
- Strategy V2 parameters are frozen and enforced at runtime (`strategy_config.py`); non-frozen backtest parameters are rejected unless `ALLOW_EXPERIMENTAL_PARAMS=true`, and such runs must be labelled experimental.
- Prior experiment outputs (P1.2–P1.6 reports) are retained unmodified. Any additional exploratory analysis must report **every** variant tried, not the best.
- Evaluation uses only outcomes that arrive after registration; historical backtests are context, not confirmation.

## 12. Known limitations accepted at registration
Delayed public Yahoo data; hand-curated holiday calendar; no stored adjusted prices; no index benchmark series; universe is 47 large caps (survivorship: constituents chosen with hindsight); modeled slippage is an assumption; broker fee schedule is unverified against the contracted broker.
