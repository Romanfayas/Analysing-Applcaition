## 2. Completed fixes

1. **Exchange evidence pipeline** (`backend/cmd/exchange_evidence`, migrations 33–35). Operator-only, append-only tables for NSE
   bhavcopies, reconciliation runs and primary-document checks; the runtime role can only read them. The secure acquisition
   pipeline was extended (NSE archive host; honest client identification: it stops on HTTP 401/403 and never impersonates a browser).
   Files whose internal trade date differs from the requested date are rejected.
2. **Provenance is not authenticity.** TLS certificate, URL and SHA-256 are recorded as *provenance*. An earlier version of
   this work passed "TLS present" as authenticity proof; that was corrected. Nothing derived from a download, HTTPS or a checksum
   can promote evidence, and `primary_document_checks` structurally cannot record a validated chain (`CHECK (chain_validated = false)`).
3. **Strict read-time eligibility** (`database.StrictResearchEligible`). A bar is research-eligible only if it is a nominal vendor bar
   or its adjustment is backed *now* by an undisputed verified factor on a `VERIFIED_EXCHANGE_PRIMARY` event. The write-time guard
   alone let bars stay released after their event was demoted.
4. **Intraday feed:** `first_received_at` (never overwritten) makes latency auditable; gap-aware recovery re-requests incomplete
   sessions inside the provider's ~7-day window; feed-health endpoint; the newest (still-forming) minute is excluded from latency
   and from forecast bars (it was being treated as complete).
5. **TrueData adapter** no longer fabricates: intraday returned an empty series labelled VALID and the quote endpoint returned
   `0.0` labelled VALID; both now fail closed and capabilities no longer advertise them.
6. **Out-of-sample strength:** edge gate v2 requires ≥ 20 independent test days (stricter only) and reports per-day and
   per-market-regime results. Model, features, Strategy V2 parameters and pinned hash unchanged (asserted by tests).
7. **Test integrity:** fixed hard-coded "exactly 23 artifacts" invariants without weakening them (baseline still exactly 23 and
   every new artifact must be unpromoted, exchange-hosted, checksummed evidence); resynced two drifted sequences in the isolated
   test database; corrected several test assertions written incorrectly during this work.

## 6. Intraday feed reliability and latency (live, 2026-11-02)

Measured from real live polls on 2026-11-02, 14:05–15:29 IST (Yahoo, 15-minute polling). Nothing is estimated.

**Session-wide (as the app reports it, `/data-quality/intraday-feed`):**

| Metric | Observations | Median | p95 | p99 | Max |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Bar receipt (finished bar → first receipt) | 3343 | 889 s | 2693 s | 2855 s | 2936 s |
| Poll freshness (age of newest finished bar at each poll) | 221 | 35.5 s | 824 s | 838 s | 887 s |

215 bars arrived as still-forming minutes and are excluded from both.

**Per poll (TCS-style batches across 50 symbols)** — needed to read the figures above honestly:

| Poll (IST) | Symbols | Freshness median / max | Bar receipt median / max | What it is |
| :-- | --: | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 14:05 | 49 | 34 s / 69 s | — | first poll after Docker restart (catch-up; excluded from bar receipt) |
| 14:10 | 43 | 27 s / 68 s | 124 s / 265 s | worker restart 5 min later |
| 14:59 | 50 | 32 s / 59 s | 1,450 s / 2,936 s | **catch-up after the Mac slept ~33 min** (host suspension, not the feed) |
| 15:14 | 49 | 33 s / 64 s | **425 s / 879 s** | the only clean steady-state 15-minute poll |
| 15:29 | 21 | 825 s / 887 s | 817 s / 887 s | closing poll: 15:16–15:28 never delivered (structural gap) |

**Reading:** the provider itself delays bars by **~30 s (max ~70 s)**. End-to-end receipt is dominated by the **15-minute polling
interval** (steady state: median ~7 min, up to ~15 min). The session-wide p95/p99 are inflated by the host sleep and the closing
gap; they are reported as measured, not trimmed. One steady-state poll is a small sample: more sessions are needed for stable
percentiles, and the measurement now accumulates automatically every trading day.

- **Structural gaps:** minutes 15:16–15:28 IST are missing on ~98% of symbol-days (provider omission; unrecoverable by re-polling).
- **Partial minutes:** the provider returns the still-forming minute; such bars are excluded from latency and from forecasts.
- **Recovery:** incomplete past sessions inside ~7 days are re-requested; older holes (e.g. 2026-10-22/23) are unrecoverable.
- **TrueData:** `NOT_CONFIGURED`. No credentials → no latency, completeness or recovery measurement is possible; the adapter has no
  intraday/real-time implementation. Nothing about TrueData is claimed beyond this.
- **Host sleep:** on 2026-11-02 the Mac slept for ~33 minutes during market hours; Docker's VM was suspended, so ingestion and
  forecasting paused (timers resumed afterwards). A machine that sleeps cannot run a market-hours pipeline reliably.
